Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

This policy applies to all SSAMOK SOFT apps and this website.

SSAMOK SOFT ("we", "us") explains how we handle information across our apps and this website. Because our apps differ, each app's page includes a data-processing summary specific to that app.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the app, we collect: an email address (for apps with an account), an advertising identifier (for apps that show ads), and purchase records, receipts, and transaction identifiers (for apps with in-app purchases). Some apps store data only on your device and collect no personal information on a server. We do not collect payment card information.

2. How we use information

We use collected information to manage accounts and verify users, operate and provide our services, handle in-app purchases and purchase history, respond to inquiries, improve our services, and deliver advertising.

3. In-app purchases

Some apps offer in-app purchases through the App Store and Google Play. Payments are handled by each store; we do not collect or store payment card information. We may process purchase-related information such as receipts and transaction identifiers for purchase validation and support.

4. Third-party services

Depending on the app, we use: Google Firebase (authentication, database, usage analytics, crash analysis), Supabase (backend), Google AdMob (ad delivery and measurement), and the App Store and Google Play (in-app purchase processing). Apps that store data only on your device do not transmit it to a server. The services each app actually uses are listed in that app's data-processing summary.

5. Advertising

Some apps may show ads through Google AdMob. An advertising identifier and similar data may be used to deliver and measure ads. You can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad personalization in your device settings.

6. Data retention & deletion

We retain your personal information while your account remains active. Account deletion requested inside an app takes effect immediately; deletion requests received by email (ssamoksoft@gmail.com) are processed within 30 days after identity verification, and we reply with the result. Information that must be kept under applicable law is retained for the required period. In some apps, report records are deleted automatically 90 days after receipt, and the identities concerned are removed earlier if the related account is deleted; copies in disaster-recovery backups expire with the backup retention cycle. App data stored only on your device is deleted when you delete the app.

7. Storage & security

Depending on the app, data is stored in the Google Firebase or Supabase cloud, or only on your device. For cloud-stored data, passwords and sensitive information are encrypted, and we maintain quarterly self-audits, access controls, access logs kept for at least six months, and intrusion prevention and detection systems.

8. Your rights

You may request to access, correct, delete, or suspend the processing of your personal information, and you can also use the privacy controls provided by the App Store and Google Play.

9. Children

Our apps are not directed to children under the age required by applicable law, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

10. Privacy officer & contact

Privacy officer: Dae-hoon Kang. For any privacy question or request, contact us at ssamoksoft@gmail.com.

11. EU & UK representative

For users in the EU/EEA and the United Kingdom, we have appointed representatives pursuant to Article 27 of the EU GDPR and Article 27 of the UK GDPR. EU representative: Prighter EU Rep GmbH, Schellinggasse 3/10, 1010 Vienna, Austria. UK representative: Prighter Ltd, 20 Mortlake High Street, London, SW14 8JN, United Kingdom. You can contact our representatives and exercise your rights via https://app.prighter.com/portal/19708232400.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page, and the revised policy takes effect when posted.
