    Announced: 2026-10-02 · Effective: 2026-10-02

    Snuumo is available to general users on Google Play and the App Store. This revised policy applies from 2026-10-02. Until then, the previous policy preserved below applies: https://www.ssamoksoft.com/privacy/snuumo/archive/before-analytics-2026-10-02/?lang=en This Privacy Policy was originally drafted in Korean. This English text is a translation of the Korean original at https://www.ssamoksoft.com/privacy/snuumo/?lang=ko, provided so that users and reviewers who do not read Korean can understand the same content. SSAMOK SOFT (싸목소프트) (the "Company") provides the memo application "Snuumo" (the "Service") and protects and processes users' personal information as set out below, in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act (개인정보 보호법, "PIPA") and other applicable laws.

    1. Personal information collected and how it is collected

    • Category | Items | Method
    • Account (required) | Email address and password (stored encrypted), or social login identifier (Google · Apple account identifier, email, provider-supplied name and profile-photo URL) | On sign-up
    • Content | Memo content (text and checklists), attached photos (compressed copies; for Pro, including originals), text recognised in photos (OCR), recognised dictation text that you save, link preview information, reminder times | When the user creates or attaches them (stored on the server for sync and backup purposes)
    • Location information | Coordinates and place names of memo locations and EXIF coordinates of attached iOS photos. Location metadata is removed from photo files before upload | Only after separate optional location-service consent and enabling Record memo location. Device location also requires OS permission; iOS photo EXIF is separate from OS location permission. Recording can be paused and consent withdrawn in settings
    • Location-usage records | Account identifier, use time, source, operation and related memo identifier; no actual coordinates, place names, text or photos | Automatically recorded during location processing under Location Information Act Article 16(2)
    • Location consent state | Current consent status, notice version, decision time and request identifiers | Processing consent and withdrawal and preventing replay of stale requests from other devices
    • Subscription information | Plan, status and expiry on the Company server; purchase history and receipts/purchase tokens verified and managed by RevenueCat | When checking subscriptions after login, subscribing to Pro or restoring purchases (payment means such as credit card details are handled by the store, such as Apple, and are not collected by the Company)
    • We do not track users for advertising or collect contacts. Google Sign-In, Maps and ML Kit SDKs may automatically process app/OS/device information, installation or SDK identifiers, feature events, errors, crashes and performance/usage information. RevenueCat processes subscription analytics and a country inferred from the IP address. The external services below explain this processing. Memo text and photo content are not used for advertising or behavioural analytics.
    • Dictation keeps audio on your device when supported by the device and current language. Only when on-device recognition is unavailable can you choose an alternative after the in-app notice, which may process audio on servers operated by Apple, Google or the speech service provider configured on your device. The Company does not upload or store original dictation audio on its servers. When you save the recognised text as a memo, it is stored on your device and stored and synchronised on the Company servers like other memo text. The provider's processing and retention of audio depend on its policies and your settings. You can type text directly without using dictation.
    • Text recognition in photos (OCR) is processed on the device. ML Kit may send Google metadata such as image format/resolution, event timing, latency and errors for diagnostics and usage statistics. These statistics do not send the OCR photo or recognised text content.
    • If a user enters a web address (URL) in a memo, the app connects directly to that website in order to generate a preview (in the course of which the user's IP address is transmitted to that website).
    • The app accesses the device calendar and reminders only when you export a memo to them. Exported content is passed to the device app and is not sent to or stored on the Company server. The Company does not read your existing events or reminders (on Android, it only checks calendar identifiers so you can select a destination calendar).
    • The Android app is configured to exclude all app data from Android automatic cloud backups and device-to-device transfers. Snuumo account sync, Pro original photo storage and manual export are separate features. Before erasing your device or reinstalling the app, check the sync status and keep a separate copy of anything not yet synced using [Settings → Data and storage → Export my data].
    • Usage analytics (optional): Help improve Snuumo by sharing app launches, screen views and usage by memo type with Google Firebase Analytics. App usage duration and in-app purchase events may also be collected. App version, device, OS, language, an app-installation identifier and a region inferred from your connection IP may also be processed. We do not send memo content, photos, search terms, memo locations, account identifiers or contacts, or use this data for advertising. All features work if you decline. You can turn collection off anytime in Settings → Security and privacy.
    • Usage analytics is off by default and starts only after you separately agree on this device. Your choice is stored locally and is not synced to your account or other devices. Declining does not restrict features. This choice is separate from existing essential processing such as sign-in and memo sync.

    2. Purposes of use

    • Creating, authenticating and managing accounts
    • Providing sync across devices, cloud backup (including Pro original backup) and the memo sharing feature
    • Location-based search (finding your memos by place name) and map display — locations recorded with memos are not used for any purpose other than this (Terms of Service, Article 10, Location-Based Services) https://www.ssamoksoft.com/privacy/snuumo/terms/
    • Verifying subscription status, providing paid features and reviewing subscription analytics
    • Responding to enquiries, giving service notices and maintaining/improving services using SDK diagnostics, performance and usage statistics

    3. Retention periods and destruction

    • Data | Retention period
    • All account data and content (memos, photos, profile, share snapshots) | Deleted without delay upon withdrawal of membership ([Settings → Security and privacy → Delete account] in the app). If the volume of data is large, the server's clean-up work continues afterwards; once completed, the data cannot be recovered
    • Memo location information | Deleted on permanent memo deletion, account deletion or withdrawal of location consent. Pausing recording alone does not erase existing locations
    • Location-usage records | Six months from the event on the device and at least six months from receipt/recording on the server, followed by expiry cleanup. Withdrawal deletes associated records without delay under Article 24(4); account deletion also deletes them
    • Location consent state and request identifiers | Retained while the account exists to enforce consent and prevent stale-device replays; deleted with the account. No coordinates, place names or memo text
    • Memos in the trash | Permanently deleted 30 days after being moved there
    • Share link snapshots | Expire and are deleted 30 days after creation (location information is not included in snapshots, and a snapshot can be deleted at any time before expiry from [Settings → Data and storage → My shared links] in the app)
    • Pro original backups (on subscription cancellation) | Retained for 90 days from the subscription expiry date (viewable and restorable), then deleted
    • Withdrawal-verification identifier (account identifier and withdrawal time) | Deleted 180 days after withdrawal. Used only to clean up the local data of another device that was still logged in but switched off, when that device connects again
    • Deletion job records (account identifier, share token, paths of files to be deleted) | Deleted 30 days after deletion is completed. Used only to check for missed deletions and to retry them
    • Records the Company is legally required to preserve | The period set by the relevant law (for example, 5 years for records of contracts and exercise of the right of withdrawal under the Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce (전자상거래 등에서의 소비자보호에 관한 법률))
    • Upon withdrawal, the local data on the device from which account deletion was carried out is also deleted, without a separate choice, and the local data on other devices logged in to the same account is deleted automatically the next time each such device connects.
    • In other words, what withdrawal erases is the account, memos, photos, profile, share snapshots and the devices' local data, and records retained in our account and memo storage are the withdrawal-verification identifier (180 days), deletion job records (30 days) and legally required records in the table above. External subscription, connection and enquiry records are handled separately as described in section 4. The retained withdrawal-verification and deletion-job records contain no memo content, photos or location information.
    • Destruction is carried out by an unrecoverable method (permanent deletion from the database and storage).
    • Optional analytics retains event-level data for 2 months and user-level data for 14 months. New activity restarts the 14-month retention period for the user's identifier. Expired data is handled through Google's periodic deletion process. These settings do not set a deletion deadline for all standard aggregate reports.

    5. Provision to third parties

    • We do not sell personal data or provide it for advertising. Section 4 distinguishes processing on our behalf from external platform features you choose. Other third-party disclosures require the necessary consent or a legal basis.

    6. Users' rights

    • Users may at any time request access to, correction, deletion, suspension of processing or portability of their personal information, or withdraw their consent. Viewing, editing and deleting memos, and deleting the account, can be done directly in the app. Users can export the account's memos, notes and folders stored on this device and available attached photos as JSON and images in a ZIP through [Settings → Data and storage → Export my data] in the app. Some content may be missing if recent changes from another device have not yet synced or photos cannot be retrieved. Original photos are included only when you select the option to include originals and the files can be retrieved. Check the completion notice and snuumo_export.json inside the ZIP for the included photo counts (included) and the omission details (omitted). Separate snapshots created for share links are not included in the export. Other requests are handled if sent to the contact address below.
    • Response deadline: the Company informs the user of the outcome within one month of receiving the request. Where the request is complex or there are many requests so that it cannot be handled within that period, the period may be extended by up to two further months; in that case the Company informs the user of the extension and the reasons for it within one month of receiving the request (GDPR Article 12(3)).
    • The Service is not intended for persons under the age of 14 (including persons below a higher minimum age where the law of the user's country or region sets one).
    • Location use requires separate optional consent. Turning off [Settings → Security and privacy → Record memo location] pauses only new recording on this device. [Settings → Security and privacy → Withdraw consent and delete locations] erases stored locations, including those in the trash, and associated usage records while keeping memo text, photos, tasks and reminders. This device applies the change immediately; the server and other devices apply it when connected and synced. The app shows offline pending status. Export my data includes location-usage records available from the device and server; server connection failures are listed in the ZIP omission details. Access, correction and notice can also be requested at support@ssamoksoft.com.
    • Withdrawing consent in Settings → Security and privacy → Usage analytics stops new collection and resets this device's Analytics data and identifiers. It does not retroactively delete existing server events or aggregate reports, nor does deleting a Snuumo account. We do not send Snuumo account identifiers to Analytics, so an email address alone may not identify past statistics. Contact support@ssamoksoft.com for access, deletion and other rights requests. You may decline or disable optional analytics and the related international processing while continuing to use other app features.

    7. Security measures

    • Encryption in transit (TLS) to Company authentication/sync servers and one-way encrypted storage of passwords
    • Per-account access control (a server policy under which only the user's own data is accessible, RLS)
    • No advertising tracking or advertising identifier collection; management of data needed to provide features
    • Minimisation of access privileges to personal information
