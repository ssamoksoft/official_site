    This page explains how to delete your account and data for the app “Kairotique” by SSAMOK SOFT. You can request deletion without reinstalling the app.

    Option 1 — Delete in the app (recommended, immediate)

    In the Kairotique app, go to Settings → Delete account. Your account and data are deleted immediately.

    Option 2 — Request by email (no app required)

    From the email address you signed up with, send a message to ssamoksoft@gmail.com with the subject “Kairotique account deletion request”. After identity verification, we will process your request within 30 days and reply with the result.

    What gets deleted

    Your account information (email, nickname, app settings) and every room you created, including all of its records, are deleted immediately. Records you left in rooms created by others are kept as part of that room's history: the content remains, but the author label changes to “(Deleted user)” and your stored display name is removed. If you want a specific record hidden from other participants, contact ssamoksoft@gmail.com.

    What is retained, and for how long

    Report records are deleted automatically 90 days after receipt; the reporter's and subject's identities are removed earlier if the account concerned is deleted. Copies in disaster-recovery backups expire with the backup retention cycle. We also ask RevenueCat, the service we use to manage subscription status, to delete your customer record when your account is deleted.

    About subscriptions

    Subscriptions (premium) are separate from account deletion. If you have an active subscription, cancel it directly in Google Play or the App Store — deleting your account does not cancel it automatically.

    Contact

    For questions about deletion, contact ssamoksoft@gmail.com.
